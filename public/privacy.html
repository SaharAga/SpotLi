Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Deliveree's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specific Disclosure for Gmail Integration: Deliveree requests access to the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.settings.basic scope solely upon user request to create an inbox forwarding filter for shipping and parcel notification emails. Deliveree never reads, scans, stores, or sells your inbox emails.

1. Who Operates Deliveree

Deliveree is an autonomous package tracking application operated for users in Israel. For privacy inquiries, data deletion, or account questions, contact: saharaga97@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect & Why

  • Account Details: Name and email provided when you sign in via Google, Microsoft, or Email/Password, used strictly for user authentication and cross-device synchronization.
  • Package & Tracking Data: Tracking numbers, carrier names, and package titles entered or imported by you, used to query carrier APIs and track status.
  • AI Smart Import: When using Smart Import with Gemini AI, pasted text is scrubbed of personal identifying information (PII) before transmission to Google's Gemini API for structured extraction.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising. Data is shared solely with necessary service infrastructure:

  • Google Cloud & Firebase: Secure database, authentication, and hosting infrastructure.
  • Postal & Courier APIs: Tracking numbers are transmitted to the relevant carrier (e.g. Israel Post, Cheetah, DHL, FedEx, UPS, Cainiao) to fetch live parcel delivery updates.

4. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the full right to view, export, or permanently delete your account and all stored package data at any time via Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account within the app.
