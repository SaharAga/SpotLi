1. Service Description

Deliveree is an autonomous package-tracking application currently in alpha. It allows users to track shipments across multiple carriers, organize deliveries, and synchronize data across devices.

2. Third-Party Carriers & Tracking Accuracy

Deliveree retrieves tracking updates directly from third-party courier and postal services. Carrier names, marks, and logos belong to their respective owners. Deliveree is not affiliated with or endorsed by any carrier and is not responsible for postal delays or inaccurate carrier updates.

3. Email Forwarding & Ingestion Integrations

When you enable automated email forwarding (such as Gmail or Outlook sync), Deliveree creates an inbox forwarding rule solely for parcel shipment emails. Deliveree does not read or store private communications outside of shipment tracking numbers and carrier updates.
